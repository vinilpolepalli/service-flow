Project: "Service Flow" - independent demo inspired by the problem space of Bernard (YC S26), agents for appliance service businesses that coordinate bookings, diagnosis, parts and technician handoffs. NOT affiliated; footer says so.

Concept: an operations board for a fictional appliance repair shop ("Demo Appliance Co"):
- ~8-10 sample repair tickets (synthetic customers/appliances: washers, fridges, ovens) flowing through stages: Booked -> Diagnosed -> Parts -> Scheduled -> Completed.
- Deterministic attention rules flag tickets: PART DELAYED (part ETA past scheduled visit), SLA RISK (promised window within 24h but stage behind), READY TO SCHEDULE (diagnosis done, part on hand). Fixed visible thresholds.
- Clicking a ticket shows a handoff timeline: each stage with timestamped synthetic entries showing what an agent coordinated (booking confirmed, diagnosis summary, part ordered with ETA, tech assigned).
- A small day-schedule panel for 2 fictional technicians.
Honesty bar: header badge "Sample data - demo"; copy states all tickets/customers are synthetic and rules are fixed-threshold illustrations; footer: "Independent demo built by Vinil Polepalli. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bernard."
UI: dark sleek, distinct accent ORANGE (not indigo/emerald/amber/sky/rose/lime/violet; make it clearly orange, not amber).
